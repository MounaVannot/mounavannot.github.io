My Erasmus in Bordeaux: too great for a nutshell

If you want the shortest version: my Erasmus was great!
If i can tell you a bit more about my experiences: my time in Bordeaux was just wonderful and flew by incredibly fast as well. I for sure had my ups and downs during my exchange, but i don’t think i would be the same person now without them.
In the last week, i was one of the last ones to stay in Bordeaux. Every day that we had to say goodbye felt like heartbreak. I once described the feeling as a colourful piece of your Erasmus is leaving your puzzle.
The experience really is made by the people you meet, the conversations you have and the adventures you are willing to face.


I was lucky to meet the base of what was about to become my friend group already in the first two weeks of my stay. It appeared that my environment was turning into exploring the German culture, since everyone around me turned out to be German. We had a good time exchanging our language similarities and differences. Sometimes it did feel a bit overwhelming to be the only one with a language barriere, since next to English there was still a lot of German spoken. But now after a while back in Belgium, it even encouraged me to start learning German on my own.

“I only feel a bigger desire to do another exchange”

Erasmus made me push my boundaries, physically and mentally. From times feeling lonely in my room to times facing mountains by bike or on a hike are truly experiences i will never forget.
The only times I don’t think about too often, are the long days at school. It was a challenge, studying completely in French, but the bigger obstacle was the amount of self study combined with often a lack of guidance. For three months, we only had days of self study obligated at campus from 9h to 17h. These were long days were you were productive in the beginning, but drained at the end. It didn’t feel good to be locked in for this amount of time, with quite no guidance of where to go. Especially the last month took its toll, but it is what it is and I’m still proud of tmy final work. To conclude, i think my friends learned me how to step out of my comfort zone mentally. My school learned me to communicate my boundaries more clearly in the future. The appearance of my room might be a reflection of how i feel, but there’s always a possibility to clean and take action in your own hands.
After these four months, i only feel a bigger desire to do another exchange. To grow further with the aspects i gained so far.
I am very grateful for this opportunity and would only recommend everyone to fully commit to your ups and downs and learn from them all.
