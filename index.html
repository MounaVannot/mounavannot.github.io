Mouna Vannot

Hello! My name is Mouna Vannot, I’m 22 years old and born in Antwerp.
I’m a student Journalism at AP Hogeschool (Antwerp), graduating in Video technics. Next to my study, you can find me at Stampmedia as a former youth representative. When I’m not talking with people or working on the next project, I’m probably doing sports like cycling or weightlifting.

I’m passionated by travelling and learning new languages. I love being social and am always open to meet new people. I have quite some experience with cultural exchanges: I just did my Erasmus in Bordeaux where I studied in French. After highschool, I did a gap year in France and Spain where I followed language courses.

Do you have a request for a video or like to collaborate? I would be happy to be creative together!
