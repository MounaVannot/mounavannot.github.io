<h2>Meeting journalists from all over the world in Perugia</h2>
If you have never been to the International Journalism Festival (IJF) in Perugia, I would firmly recommend you to go there.
It's a complete free and very enriching place that breathes professional experiences.
The city is divided in approximately eight different buildings where you can listen to a discussion, someone's eye-opening biography or the results of years long research. 
What was surprising to me, is that every conversation is linked to journalism in a completely different way. Where one group talks about mental and physical health of journalists, the other one reports about the impact of AI in the field or the journalistic journey of the Gisele Pelicot legal process.

In Perugia, journalism is combined with beautiful views. Mountains and nature surround the student city centre. With its rich buildings and campuses, these four days form the base of networking. Sport journalist, researchers, videographers and editors-in-chief come together to share their knowledge.<br>
As a student myself, these four days were a real added value to my future career. I am convinced I will go back there once my agenda allows me to, and if not I'll make time for this.<br>
<div style="text-align:center;">
  <blockquote>
   <h3> “She spoke with so much passion that she made me realise becoming an international researcher is possible”</h3>
  </blockquote>
</div>
I remember this woman with pink hair who made a big impression on me. Even from in the crowd, she was outstanding everyone else. Later on, I came to the knowledge that she was a panel speaker herself. Not only her looks were intriguing to me, it was the way she talked about journalism that made everyone in the audience melt. She spoke with so much passion that she made me realise becoming an international researcher is possible.<br> 
With five other students, we were based in a house half an hour away by bus from the city centre. I recommend staying a bit further to enjoy the suburbs from Perugia and avoid extra costs. If you would ever like to go there, you are very welcome to join me!<br>
