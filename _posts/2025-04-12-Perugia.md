<h2>Meeting journalists from all over the world in Perugia</h2>
If you have never been to the International Journalism Festival (IJF) in Perugia, i would firmly recommend you to go there.
It's a complete free and very enriching place that breathes professional experiences at every place you go to.
The city is divided in approximately eight different buildings where you can listen to a discussion, someone's eye-opening biography or the results of years long research.
